KolKol Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how BlackBox Industries PTY handles information when you use the KolKol mobile application to set up, monitor and control a compatible hot-tub controller.

1. Information used by KolKol

KolKol uses device information to set up and operate your hot tub. The app does not require a personal account or collect personal profile information for these features.

2. Local setup and permissions

During setup, the app sends the Wi-Fi details you enter directly to your controller over Bluetooth. The controller uses these details to join your network.

KolKol uses Bluetooth and Wi-Fi permissions for device discovery and setup. Android may require location permission for nearby wireless scanning. You can manage these permissions in your phone's settings.

3. Remote control

Remote control uses an online service to connect the app to your hot tub.

4. How information is used and shared

The online service and its hosting providers process information needed for remote control.

5. Security and data handling

Wi-Fi setup takes place over a direct Bluetooth connection to your controller.

6. Retention and deletion

Saved app settings can be removed by clearing the app's storage through Android settings. This only removes settings stored on your phone.

The remote control service does not keep device or connection information after processing it.

7. Your choices

You can revoke app permissions in your device settings, stop using remote connectivity, or clear local app storage. Some features will no longer work if the required access is disabled.

8. Children

KolKol is intended for adults responsible for operating compatible hot-tub equipment.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when functionality or information-handling practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate notice.
