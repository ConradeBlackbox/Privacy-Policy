KolKol Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how BlackBox Industries PTY ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use the KolKol mobile app to set up and control a compatible hot-tub controller. For privacy questions or requests, email info@blackbox.co.za.

1. Information KolKol uses

KolKol does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or other personal profile information. To operate your hot tub, it uses only:

  • your controller's identifier and settings;
  • temperatures, schedules, alarms and status reported by the controller; and
  • the Wi-Fi network name and password you enter during setup, which are sent only to your controller.

2. How information is used and shared

This information is used only to set up your controller, send it commands and show its status in the app. For remote control, commands and status pass through our messaging server (an MQTT broker) and its hosting provider. Like any internet service, the server sees your connection's IP address while it relays messages.

We do not sell information, use it for advertising or share it with anyone else, except where required by law.

3. Secure data handling procedures

We protect personal and sensitive user data with the following procedures:

  • Device pairing: Your phone must be paired with your controller over a direct Bluetooth connection before it can control it. The controller only accepts commands from paired phones.
  • Message validation: Each controller has its own messaging topic. Messages are checked with a key set up during pairing, and the controller ignores any message that fails the check.
  • Wi-Fi credentials stay local: Your Wi-Fi password is sent only to your controller, over a direct Bluetooth connection. It is never sent to or stored on our servers.
  • No personal data in messages: Remote messages carry only controller commands and operating information. They contain no names, accounts, Wi-Fi passwords or location.
  • No message storage: Our messaging server relays messages for delivery only. It does not store messages, IP addresses or connection logs.
  • Private on-device storage: App settings are kept in KolKol's private app storage on your phone, which other apps cannot access.
  • Limited permissions: Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and, on some Android versions, location permissions are used only to find and set up nearby controllers. KolKol does not track or store your location.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. If you suspect unauthorised access to your controller, contact us at info@blackbox.co.za.

4. Data retention and deletion

  • App settings stay on your phone until you clear KolKol's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.
  • Settings on the controller, including Wi-Fi details, stay there until you change them or reset the controller.
  • Our messaging server does not keep device or connection information after delivering a message.
  • To ask about or request deletion of information, email info@blackbox.co.za with the subject "KolKol privacy request".

5. Your choices

You can revoke app permissions in your phone's settings, stop using remote control, or clear KolKol's storage. Some features will stop working if the required access is turned off.

6. Children

KolKol is intended for adults responsible for operating compatible hot-tub equipment and is not aimed at children under 18.

7. Your rights

You have rights under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and may raise concerns with the Information Regulator.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or our practices change. The date above shows the latest revision.
